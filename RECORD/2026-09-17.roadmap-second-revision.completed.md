## 2026-09-17 00:45 — Second revision drafted

Drafted `ROADMAP/2026-09-17/` as a new dated directory; `ROADMAP/2026-09-16/` is
untouched, per `AGENTS.md`. Eleven files: overview with the Gantt, seven phase
files, a nice-to-haves file, and the open questions.

What changed against the first revision:

- **Phase 0 is marked done** (`ws`, `ci0`), and `auth` is `active` with a 3 day
  remainder rather than its original 7: the flow is written and unit-tested, and
  what is left is verification against GOG. That is why the whole chart starts
  earlier than the previous revision's.
- **A UI phase was added** (`04-ui.md`): `spike`, `grid`, `search`, `actions` and
  `feed`, from the requirement recorded in
  `RECORD/2026-09-16.controller-ui-requirement.completed.md` — cover grid, badges,
  per-cover install progress, search with an on-screen keyboard, install and
  uninstall. `feed` moved here from the saves phase, since the pre/post launch
  screens are modes of the same UI binary.
- **The UI spike is scheduled first**, in parallel with `auth`, ahead of everything
  it depends on. Gamescope focus, gamepad mapping and legibility on a TV cannot be
  judged from a desktop, and discovering a problem in December would be late.
- **Three new tasks in core and on Linux**: `art` (cover art fetch and cache),
  `state` (the installed-games registry the badge, uninstall and the wrapper all
  need) and `uninst` (the uninstall path, which never deletes saves).
- **`e2e` was added before M5.** The previous revision's M4 claimed "fresh mini PC,
  one line, …" with no task that proved it.
- **Milestones renumbered**: a UI milestone is now M3, so the old M3/M4/M5 are
  M4/M5/M6 here. The overview carries a mapping column, because records written
  before today use the old numbering.
- **Nice to haves live in their own file** (`09-nice-to-haves.md`) with the task
  each can only start after: the QR login handoff, a SteamGridDB art fallback,
  update detection, and shared-machine polish. None of them gate a milestone.
- **Open questions**: 4 (tile or CLI) and the login flow are settled and listed as
  such; two new ones were added — the Rust floor versus `eframe` 0.36, and where
  cover art comes from. Question 3 (GOG client credentials) is now flagged as
  blocking in practice, since nothing is baked in and `gamestore login` needs the
  user to supply credentials.
- **The chart excludes the winter holidays**, not only weekends: 24 and 25
  December, 31 December, 1 and 6 January.

Milestone targets that follow from the dependencies: M1 2026-11-10, M2 2026-11-26,
M3 2026-12-09, M4 2027-01-05, M5 2027-01-11, M6 2027-02-04. The UI is deliberately
off the critical path — `grid` and `search` can be finished in October while the
downloader is still being written, because the grid only needs the catalog, the art
cache and the registry; what gates M3 is `actions`, which needs a working install.

### Verification

- The Gantt was rendered with `@mermaid-js/mermaid-cli`: 33 tasks, 6 milestones, 8
  sections, 2 done, 1 active, 2 critical.
- The dates in the rendered chart were then decoded from the SVG and compared
  against an independent working-day scheduler
  (`excludes weekends` plus the five holiday dates) — every task start and all six
  milestone dates agree with the milestone table in the overview. The one
  systematic difference is cosmetic: where a task's last day is a Friday, Mermaid
  draws the bar ending on the Saturday while the scheduler reports the following
  Monday; the successor's start is identical either way.

  2026-12-30 as it would without them.
