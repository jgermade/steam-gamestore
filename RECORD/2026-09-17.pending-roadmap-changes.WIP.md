## 2026-09-17 01:15 — Why this file exists

Decisions are recorded as they are made, but a roadmap revision is a dated
snapshot: per `AGENTS.md` its task states are only refreshed when a newer revision
is drafted, and existing dated directories are never rewritten. So between
revisions the plan and the decisions drift apart on purpose.

Rather than draft a revision per decision, decisions accumulate and the next
revision folds them in together — most likely once `auth` is closed with a run on
the mini PC, since that run answers several open questions at once.

This file is the accumulator: **one entry per decision that the next revision has
to absorb**, appended as they land. When that revision is drafted, it is renamed to
`.completed.md` and the new revision's own record takes over.
