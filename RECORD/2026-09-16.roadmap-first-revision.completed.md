## 2026-09-16 — First roadmap revision drafted

Turned the design notes in `docs/gog-installer.md` into the first dated roadmap
revision, `ROADMAP/2026-09-16/`, with nine Markdown files:

- `00-overview.md` — goal, scope, phase index, milestones M1–M5, the Mermaid
  Gantt chart for the whole plan, and the critical path.
- `01-foundations.md` — Rust workspace skeleton and CI build matrix.
- `02-core.md` — GOG OAuth2, token storage, catalog, manifest parsing, chunked
  downloader, integrity check and resume.
- `03-platform-linux.md` — non-Steam appid (CRC32), `shortcuts.vdf`,
  `CompatToolMapping`, silent InnoSetup install through Proton, launch wrapper.
- `04-saves.md` — Ludusavi CLI integration, GOG-to-Ludusavi title mapping,
  pre/post launch screens.
- `05-save-sync.md` — evaluate rclone vs Syncthing vs a custom backend, then
  implement the chosen transport.
- `06-distribution.md` — release workflow, `install.sh`, `self-update`.
- `07-platform-windows.md` — native installer runner, Windows wrapper, Steam
  shortcuts, `install.ps1`.
- `08-open-questions.md` — the seven decisions this revision leaves open.

Schedule: `ws` starts 2026-09-16, the chart excludes weekends, and the milestone
targets that follow from the dependencies are M1 2026-11-19, M2 2026-12-07,
